## 3. Uses that require prior written permission

Unless a legal exception applies, prior written permission is required to:

- use AETHERON branding as the name or branding of another product, service,
  company, application, website, domain, social account, or repository;
- use the AETHERON name or logo in a way that suggests sponsorship,
  endorsement, partnership, certification, or official affiliation;
- reproduce, adapt, recolor, or create a confusingly similar version of
  official AETHERON logos or brand artwork for commercial or promotional use;
- register or use a confusingly similar company name, product name, domain,
  social handle, app listing, or marketplace listing;
- use AETHERON brand assets in advertising, merchandise, paid services,
  resale, or distribution;
- publish an unofficial fork or derivative product under the AETHERON name
  or visual identity.

Permission must be explicit and in writing. Repository visibility, a code
download, a contribution, or silence does not constitute brand permission.

## 4. Uses generally allowed without implying affiliation

This policy is not intended to prohibit fair, truthful, non-confusing
references permitted by applicable law. For example:

- accurately identifying AETHERON as the subject of a review, article,
  comparison, academic discussion, or security report;
- referring to the project by name when discussing compatibility or
  interoperability, where the reference is necessary and lawful;
- linking to the official repository without copying brand artwork.

Such references must be truthful, proportionate, and must not imply that the
speaker is the owner, developer, sponsor, or authorized representative.

## 5. Brand integrity

Do not:

- alter official artwork in a way that misrepresents the project;
- make false claims about official status, certification, registration,
  security, capabilities, or endorsement;
- use the brand for fraud, phishing, malware, impersonation, or unlawful
  activity;
- present an independent product or service as an official AETHERON release;
- register a mark, domain, account, or listing that is likely to confuse
  users about the source or affiliation of AETHERON.

## 7. Requesting permission or reporting misuse

For permission requests, identify the exact mark or asset, proposed use,
territory, duration, audience, and whether the use is commercial. Contact
the repository owner through the contact route published on the official
GitHub profile or repository.

For a suspected misuse, provide the relevant URL, screenshots or other
evidence, the mark or asset involved, and a short explanation. Do not send
passwords, API keys, or other secrets.
